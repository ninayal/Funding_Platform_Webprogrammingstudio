Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Làng & Co. collects, why it is used, when it may be shared, and the choices available to users of our website, marketplace, account, wishlist, community, and customer-support features.

1. Scope of this Policy

This Policy applies when you browse the Làng & Co. website, create an account, place an order, save products, submit reviews or community content, contact support, or otherwise interact with our online services.

Third-party websites and services linked from our website have their own privacy practices. This Policy does not control how those third parties process information.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, gender selection, and password.
  • Order information, such as products purchased, delivery address, billing details, and transaction status.
  • Content you submit, including reviews, comments, wishlist entries, forum posts, and support messages.
  • Marketing preferences and responses to surveys, promotions, or feedback requests.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser information, IP address, language, and general location derived from network information.
  • Pages viewed, links selected, referring pages, access times, and interaction data.
  • Cookie identifiers and similar technical information used to maintain sessions and remember preferences.

Information from service providers

We may receive limited transaction, delivery, fraud-prevention, analytics, or authentication information from payment providers, shipping carriers, social sign-in providers, and other vendors that support the service.

3. How we use information

We use information where reasonably necessary to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Process payments, orders, shipping, returns, and refunds.
  • Provide wishlists, product recommendations, community features, and customer support.
  • Authenticate users, prevent fraud, protect accounts, and maintain website security.
  • Improve website performance, product selection, accessibility, and user experience.
  • Send service messages, order updates, and—where permitted—marketing communications.
  • Comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We may use essential cookies to keep you signed in, maintain a shopping session, remember settings, and protect the website. Analytics cookies may help us understand how visitors use pages and where improvements are needed.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may prevent account, cart, checkout, or preference features from working correctly.

5. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Payment processors that complete and verify transactions.
  • Shipping carriers and fulfillment partners that deliver orders.
  • Hosting, analytics, email, security, and customer-support providers working on our behalf.
  • Artisan or marketplace partners when information is needed to prepare or fulfill a specific order.
  • Authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • A successor organization in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of business assets.

Service providers are expected to use information only for the services they perform and to apply appropriate safeguards.

6. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including account operation, order records, customer support, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and dispute resolution.

Retention periods differ by data type. Some records may remain in secure backups or be retained where required by law, even after an account is closed.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. However, no online system, transmission method, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should use a strong, unique password and avoid sharing account credentials. Contact us promptly if you believe that your account or personal information has been compromised.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on applicable law and the nature of your request, you may be able to:

  • Access or receive a copy of certain personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete account information.
  • Request deletion of information that is no longer required.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails by using the unsubscribe option in the message.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Some requests may be limited where information must be retained for transactions, security, legal obligations, or legitimate business records.

9. Children's privacy

The website is not designed for children who cannot legally consent to online data processing in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly request personal information from such children without appropriate permission from a parent or guardian.

10. International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use reasonable contractual, technical, or organizational safeguards for international transfers.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, business practices, or legal requirements. The updated version will display a revised effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the website or email.
